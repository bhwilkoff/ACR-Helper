Testing ACR Helper

Until Google approves ACR Helper for everyone, each tester installs it once from Google Cloud. Use a computer and Chrome, signed in with the Google account you were invited with.

Once, before the call (about two minutes)

  1. Open ACR Helper's test deployment. If Google asks you to agree to the Cloud terms, agree. If a message says some information could not be loaded, close it.
  2. Scroll to Deployments and press Install beside acr-helper. Google says the deployment was installed.

In the Meet call

  1. Open Activities (the shapes icon at the bottom right), then Add-ons, then ACR Helper.
  2. When the chair starts the review, Meet shows Join activity. Press it.
  3. In the side panel, press Sign in with Google. The first time only, Google's window asks you to allow access to the Drive files you use with this app; press Continue.
  4. The first time only, Google's file picker opens in the side panel. Select the TEST spreadsheet and press Select. If it is not listed, close the picker and give the chair the email address the side panel shows; the chair adds you with Add someone, then press Choose the spreadsheet.

The test uses a spreadsheet of made-up applicants. If something looks wrong, tell the chair what you pressed and what you saw.
