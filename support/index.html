Support

Write to benwilkoff@gmail.com. Say what you pressed and what the add-on showed; never send applicant information.

Getting started

  1. Join the committee's Google Meet™ call from a computer, in Chrome.
  2. Open Activities, then Add-ons, then ACR Helper, and press Sign in with Google. Use the account the committee's spreadsheet is shared with.
  3. The first time only, Google's file picker asks you to choose the committee's spreadsheet. Select it and press Select.
  4. When the chair starts a review, press the activity icon at the top right of Meet, then Join activity.

If something is wrong

  • "This review's spreadsheet isn't shared with you." Ask the chair to share the spreadsheet with the Google account you signed in with.
  • "ACR Helper needs permission to open the review spreadsheet." Sign in again and allow access on Google's screen.
  • The file picker shows no spreadsheet. The spreadsheet isn't shared with the account you signed in with; ask the chair.
  • The main stage does not open. The call's host settings must let contributors share add-on activities.
