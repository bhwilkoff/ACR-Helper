ACR Helper

A Google Meet™ add-on for an admissions review committee that decides on one applicant at a time, together, from a shared Google Sheets™ spreadsheet.

What it does

  • Shows one applicant from the committee's spreadsheet on Meet's main stage for everyone in the call, with links to the pages a reviewer needs.
  • Lets each member vote privately. Votes stay hidden until the chair reveals them, so each person's own judgment reaches the room first.
  • Writes the committee's decision into the applicant's row, with a note naming who recorded it and when.

Who can use it

Only people whose own Google account can open the committee's spreadsheet. ACR Helper checks this with Google every time it opens and keeps no list of its own. Someone in the call without access sees nothing from the sheet.

What it never does

  • It never copies the spreadsheet anywhere. Rows are read from Google into the browser of the person looking at them, and nowhere else.
  • It never stores votes.
  • It never ranks, scores, or recommends a decision. People decide.
